Chinaslop Tracker - Native Windows

1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder.
2. Open Chinaslop Tracker.exe. Keep the assets folder beside it.
3. Add your HoYoLAB and/or SKPORT connections in Settings.

This app runs independently; Node.js and a browser are not required.
The native app has separate account storage and history from older tracker apps.
Windows Credential Manager remembers its encryption key.

This executable is unsigned. Windows may show an unknown publisher warning.
Automatic refresh and check-in require the app to remain running.

For a credential-free preview, launch the executable with --demo.
Source and documentation: https://github.com/eltru892/chinaslop-tracker
